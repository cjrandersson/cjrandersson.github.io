About

CJ Robin Andersson

I’m Robin. My background sits somewhere between technology, art, music and design. More than a decade in the tech industry has taught me to look closely at how systems work, where they fail and how people actually experience them.

Alongside that career, I’ve followed an independent creative path, driven more by curiosity than by a fixed discipline. I enjoy learning unfamiliar things, finding connections between ideas that do not usually sit together and turning loose possibilities into something tangible.

I’m equally interested in entrepreneurship, marketing and product thinking—how an idea earns attention, develops an identity and finds the right form for the people it is meant to reach. I value clarity, strong character and leaving a little room for the unexpected.
